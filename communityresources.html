About This Map

This map showcases cooperatives and solidarity economy organizations across New York City, with a focus on Bedford-Stuyvesant and surrounding neighborhoods.

Categories include:

• Community Gardens - Green spaces managed by local residents
• Credit Unions - Community-owned financial institutions
• Community Land Trusts (CLTs) - Democratic land ownership models
• Food Co-ops - Member-owned grocery stores
• Housing Co-ops - Resident-owned housing
• Worker Co-ops - Employee-owned businesses
• CSAs - Community Supported Agriculture programs
• Energy Co-ops - Community-owned renewable energy
• Community Fridges - Free food distribution points

Data Source: This data comes from CEANYC's Seeding Solidarity map. For more information, visit gocoopnyc.org
