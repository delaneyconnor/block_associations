# Future Map Ideas & Features

This document contains ideas for additional maps and features you can add to your Bed Stuy Community Maps project.

---

## 🎉 Block Party Map

### Concept
An interactive map showing upcoming and past block parties in Bedford-Stuyvesant.

### Key Features to Include:
- **Event Markers**: Color-coded pins for upcoming vs. past events
- **Date/Time Display**: Clear indication of when each party is happening
- **RSVP/Interest**: Button to express interest or RSVP
- **Photo Gallery**: Upload photos from past block parties
- **Permit Status**: Show whether permit is approved
- **Filters**: Filter by date range, street, or type of event

### Data to Collect:
- Event name
- Date and time
- Street location (address range)
- Organizer/Contact info
- Expected attendance
- Activities planned (music, food, games, etc.)
- Photos (optional)

### Implementation Ideas:
1. **Time-based styling**:
   - Future events: Bright, colorful markers
   - Today: Pulsing/animated marker
   - Past events: Muted/gray markers

2. **Calendar integration**:
   - Add to Google Calendar button
   - iCal export

3. **Community engagement**:
   - Allow neighbors to pledge to bring food/supplies
   - Comment section for coordination

A comprehensive directory of essential services and community resources in Bed Stuy.

### Categories to Include:

#### 1. **Food Security**
- Food pantries
- Community fridges
- Soup kitchens
- Farmers markets
- SNAP enrollment sites

#### 2. **Healthcare**
- Hospitals & urgent care
- Community health centers
- Mental health services
- Dental clinics
- Pharmacies

#### 3. **Education & Youth**
- Libraries
- After-school programs
- Tutoring centers
- Youth centers
- Playgrounds

#### 4. **Housing & Legal**
- Housing assistance
- Legal aid
- Tenant rights organizations
- Homeless services

#### 5. **Recreation**
- Parks
- Community gardens
- Sports facilities
- Cultural centers

#### 6. **Safety & Support**
- Police precincts
- Fire stations
- Domestic violence support
- Substance abuse services

### Key Features:
- **Category filters**: Toggle different resource types
- **Hours of operation**: Show if open now

### Data to Collect:
- Organization name

- Address
- Phone number
- Website
- Hours of operation
- Services offered
- Languages spoken
- Accessibility features
- Last verified date

## Technical Considerations

### Data Management
- **Google Sheets**: Good for starting, easy to edit
- **Airtable**: More powerful, better for complex data
- **Database**: Consider PostgreSQL + PostGIS for scaling

### Hosting Options
- **GitHub Pages**: Free, easy for static sites
- **Netlify**: Free tier, custom domains
- **Vercel**: Great for modern web apps

### Future Tech Stack Ideas
- **Frontend Framework**: React, Vue, or Svelte for complex interactions
- **Backend**: Node.js/Express for API, user accounts
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with PostGIS extension
- **Authentication**: Auth0 or Firebase for user accounts
