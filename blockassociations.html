About This Map

This is an interactive map designed to help residents of Bedford-Stuyvesant locate and connect with block associations.

How to use this map:

• Click on any dot to view block association information

• Red dots are active associations (hosted a block party in 2022 or later)

• Orange dots are recent associations (last active 2018–2021)

• Grey dots are dormant associations (last active before 2018)

• Use the search box to find an association by street or name

This map is a work in progress. To provide feedback or ideas, contact delaneyconnor1@gmail.com
